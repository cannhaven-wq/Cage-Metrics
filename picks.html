This page is research, not advice. One model drives this page: Value. It prices every UFC fight on its own, then compares its number to the market. When its win probability beats the price by enough, the fight shows up here with the Kelly stake math — the formula that calculates an "optimal" stake size given a probability and a price. The number is what Kelly returns; whether you bet anything, and how much, is your call. A second model, Fight IQ, never sees a betting line — its chip on each pick tells you whether a pure tape read lands on the same fighter.

The edge number is the gap between the model's win probability and what the price implies, measured against the best current price we track (opening line if no live price yet). Bigger gap = the model thinks the market is more wrong. The track record above is simulated: the model was frozen, then graded only on fights after its training cutoff — fights it had never seen. The live record started July 2026 and is 0-1 so far — the first settled bet lost. Simulated and live are labeled separately and always will be. Expect real-world results to run below simulation once slippage and book limits bite. The Proof Center has the full breakdown if you want the receipts.
