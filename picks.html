This page is research, not advice. One engine drives this page, shown through its Value face. It prices every UFC fight on its own, then compares its number to the vig-free market consensus. When its win probability beats the price by at least 4 points, the fight shows up here with the Kelly stake math — the formula that calculates an "optimal" stake size given a probability and a price. The number is what Kelly returns; whether you bet anything, and how much, is your call. The same engine's Fight IQ face never sees a betting line — its chip on each pick tells you whether the pure tape read lands on the same fighter.

The edge number is the gap between the model's win probability and what the price implies, measured against the best current price we track (the locked publish price if no live price yet). Bigger gap = the model thinks the market is more wrong. The track record above is mostly simulated: the engine walked forward through history, only ever betting fights it had not trained on, graded at real closing prices. The live record started July 2026 and is still tiny. Simulated and live are labeled separately and always will be. Expect real-world results to run below simulation once slippage and book limits bite. The Proof Center has the full breakdown if you want the receipts.
