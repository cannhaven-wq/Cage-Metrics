Did we beat the closing price?

The toughest test of a betting model: we take -150, it closes at -180, we got the better price. Do our picks do that over and over?
Every live Value pick locks its price the moment we post it, and we compare it to the closing price after the card. That sample is still too small to mean anything, so we're not going to dress it up as proof — these numbers go up here once 100+ locked picks have both a posted price and a closing price on record. Until then, judge us on the graded record above.

How to read this honestly

  • Accuracy alone is not enough. Betting the closing favorite every time gets you to ≈68% at closing odds — for free. Our engine lands around 61% without ever seeing a betting line, and we publish that number instead of dressing it up. Fight IQ's claim is independence; Value's claim is price — it only bets where the model and the market genuinely disagree.

  • Model output, not guarantees. A 70% pick loses 3 times out of 10. Nothing here is betting advice.
  • Samples matter. Anything under ~100 picks in a bucket is noise. We show the counts so you can discount accordingly.
