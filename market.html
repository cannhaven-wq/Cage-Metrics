The board

Open a row to see every book's current quote on that fight. Best price is the best American number in the data, and nothing else decides that ordering — CFL takes no sportsbook money to move a book up this board.
How to read it. "Consensus" is the median across sportsbooks of each book's own de-vigged price — the book's margin removed, so it reads as a probability rather than a payout. "Market move" compares that consensus to our first broad capture — the first time we had the fight priced by three or more sportsbooks at once — and it uses only the books quoting at both ends. That matters: on most cards we see one book before we see six, and comparing one book then against six now would report the arrival of five sportsbooks as a market move. Where fewer than three books line up on both ends we print a dash and say so, rather than a number we could not defend. Open a row to see the books it used. We do not call this an opener, because we have never seen one: our record starts when we start watching. Prices are the last we captured, not live quotes. Always check the sportsbook before you act on anything here.

What this becomes

Market Lab is the part of Cannon Fight Lab we are building out first. Everything on this page is free and stays free. The work queued behind it is the tooling a researcher would keep open through fight week:

  • Movement charts per fight, not just the endpoints
  • Historical odds beyond the current card window
  • "What changed since you last looked", per account
  • Price-target and movement alerts, and fight watchlists
  • Stale-price detection across books

None of that is behind a paywall today, because none of it is built yet. When it is, the free board above does not shrink to make room for it.
