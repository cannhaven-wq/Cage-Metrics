2. No prediction, no guarantee

What our numbers mean

The statistics, win rates, and implied win percentages published on this site describe historical patterns in completed UFC fights. Past performance — of a fighter, of a stance, of an age group, of any measured signal — does not predict future results.

A fighter described as a 62% favorite under our model loses 38% of the time historically when that same set of conditions held. Single-fight outcomes have substantial random variance that no statistical model can eliminate. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling you something.

We make no representation, warranty, or guarantee — express or implied — that any statistic on this site will accurately predict any individual fight outcome.

3. Data accuracy

Best effort, not perfection

Historical fight data is sourced from publicly available records and refreshed on a regular automated schedule. We make a good-faith effort to ensure accuracy, but the data may contain errors introduced at the source, by the scraping process, by data-type conversions, or by gaps in coverage for older fights.

If you spot what looks like an error, let us know. We treat substantiation questions seriously and correct documented errors as quickly as we can.

See the methodology page for a full breakdown of how each statistic on this site is calculated, what the sample size is, and what the known limitations are.
