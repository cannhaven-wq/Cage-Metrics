Gamble responsibly

Sports betting can be addictive and harmful. If you choose to gamble, please follow these guidelines:

  • Set a budget before you bet, and never exceed it.
  • Never gamble money you can't afford to lose.
  • Never bet to chase losses. If you've lost, walk away.
  • Don't gamble under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or strong emotion.
  • Take regular breaks. Gambling should be one part of a balanced life, never the center of it.
  • Know the warning signs: betting more than you intended, hiding bets from family, gambling to escape problems, borrowing money to bet.

If you need help

If you or someone you know may have a gambling problem, free and confidential help is available right now:

  • National helpline: 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537)
  • Online resources: National Council on Problem Gambling
  • Self-exclusion: Most legal sportsbooks offer voluntary self-exclusion programs. Use them if you need to.
