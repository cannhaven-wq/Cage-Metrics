# BT-001 — Historical UFC betting evaluation: results

**2026-10-21. Internal research.** Nothing here goes on a public page, and no
figure from it may appear on the site as an edge or a return (CLAUDE.md,
D-011, PRED-001 §9). Plan: [`BACKTEST_PLAN_2026-10-21.md`](BACKTEST_PLAN_2026-10-21.md),
committed before any result (`cf96ead3`), with Amendment A1 (`57b26569`) added
on the owner's instruction before any result.

## Plain verdict

**We cannot say whether any strategy beats the market, and this study does not
claim one does or doesn't.** That is not because a strategy lost. It is because
CFL has never stored a price a bettor could really have taken close to the bell
on a fight we are allowed to look at.

**Why, in one sentence:** prices a bettor could actually bet, captured at a
known time before the fight, start on 19 September 2026. Every settled fight
since then is in the locked-away test period, so the number of fights we may
score is **zero**.

What we *can* say, without looking at any result:

- **A single sportsbook charges about 4% across the two sides** (median 4.2%
  on the 17 October card). Before any skill, a bettor starts about 4 cents
  down per dollar.
- **Shopping for the best price across six or seven books halves that cost**,
  to about 2.6% inside the last three hours. The best price is about 1.6%
  better than the typical book's.
- **Even a clean test would need a lot of fights.** To show a strategy makes 5
  cents per dollar on near-even fights, under the bar we fixed in advance,
  takes about 5,400 bets. A 10-cent edge takes about 1,300. The usable sample we
  hold today is 58 fights, and those are sealed.

## 1. Coverage of executable odds (required first)

**Executable** means: a real sportsbook (`v_odds_books_sportsbooks`, which
excludes the BFO consensus, the CFL consensus row, Polymarket and Kalshi), not
live, both sides quoted by the same book at the same capture instant, and
captured strictly before the fight's best-known start
(`v_fight_start_best.start_at`). Where no provider start time exists that view
falls back to **00:00 UTC on the event date**, so a quote counts only if it was
taken before the card's day began. No price values and no outcomes were read
to build this table. Source: Q2 and Q3 in
[`coverage_queries.sql`](coverage_queries.sql); per card in
[`data/actionable_coverage_by_card.csv`](data/actionable_coverage_by_card.csv).

## 4. Holdout exclusions applied

- **No outcome dated on or after 2025-01-01 was read**, in any query. No query
  in this study selected `winner_id`, `won`, `pnl_usd` or any result column for
  those dates.
- **`win_model_locks` was never queried.**
- Sealed-month queries (Q1–Q3) return counts and timestamps only, no prices.
  Prices were read only for cards on or after 2026-10-12 (Q4) and for BFO
  consensus before 2025 (Q5). One additional pre-result query listed BFO
  consensus rows for 2024-06-01 → 06-10 (DEV) to learn the row shape.
- Models excluded for having no honest unsealed window: v4, v5 (test from
  2025-01-01), v6 (declared from 2021-01-01 but trained through 2024-12-31, so
  its honest window is 2025+), the engine (trained through 2026-07).
- Disclosed in Amendment A1: before the owner's instruction arrived, one chunk
  of DEV rows (853 fights, 2007-era ids 757–1665, prices and outcomes, all
  before 2025) was pulled into scratch space. Nothing was computed from it; it
  was deleted and never committed.
- Ambiguity resolved toward excluding more: the 2026-10-17 card is not sealed by
  PRED-001's partition table, but the Researcher role may read no outcome on or
  after 2025-01-01 and PRED-002 is collecting on that card, so it was excluded.

## 5. What would make this testable

**Option A — wait (free).** W3 capture is running. Every future card adds about
12 actionable fights. Using them for a betting test is consistent with PRED-001
only if a strategy is registered and its bets are locked before each card,
which is exactly what CLV-001 and PRED-001 already provide for. At the rates in
§3 that is years, not months.

**Option B — buy historical per-book odds for the DEV years (fits the $30).**

- **What:** The Odds API, historical odds endpoint, sport
  `mma_mixed_martial_arts`, market `h2h`, region `us`, for every UFC card from
  mid-2020 to 2024-12-31. Before 2025 only: buying 2025-onward history would buy
  sealed data, which no researcher may use.
- **Why it would help:** it replaces the non-executable BFO consensus with
  timestamped prices from named books, both sides, at chosen minutes before each
  bout, so strategies a–e could be run at real prices and real timing on about
  190 cards (~2,300 fights). That is enough to detect a large edge (about +10% at
  near-even prices) and not a small one (§3).
- **Cost:** one month of the provider's smallest paid plan. From memory, and
  **unverified** because the provider's site is unreachable from this sandbox:
  about **$30/month for 20,000 credits**; a historical call costs **10 credits
  per market per region** and returns every listed MMA event at that instant;
  snapshots from **June 2020** at 10-minute steps (5-minute from late 2022).
  Eight snapshots per card (one opener-side, the rest through the card) is
  about 80 credits a card, about 15,000 for 190 cards. Third-party pages found
  by search agree on the 10× historical cost and the 2020 start but disagree on
  which plans include history ([sportsgameodds.com](https://sportsgameodds.com/compare/the-odds-api),
  [oddspapi.io](https://oddspapi.io/blog/the-odds-api-free-tier-limits)). Check
  the plan page before paying.
- **Caveats the owner should hear before buying:** the Factor Lab chose its
  factors on data that includes these years, and v1–v3's 2023–24 record is
  already published, so a positive DEV result would be non-confirmatory and
  would license only a prospective, locked test. Spending money is L3
  (`CRITICAL_GATES.md`); this session made no purchase.
