# T-086 — Keeping DWCS out of UFC records: plan for review (2026-10-13)

**Superseded 2026-10-13 by [`T-086_MIGRATION_PLAN_2026-10-13.md`](T-086_MIGRATION_PLAN_2026-10-13.md)** — the complete plan for final approval. Kept as the review draft the owner approved in principle.

**Nothing here has been applied. No fight has been deleted or changed.**

## The problem in one paragraph

`events` holds **"DWCS 10.3" (2026-08-25)**, and `fights` holds its 5 bouts
(ids 49307–49311). Those bouts happened. They are Dana White's Contender Series
fights, not UFC fights. Because they sit in `fights` like any UFC card, 10
fighters each get one extra "UFC" fight. Nicholas Galanti, who is on the
2026-10-17 card, therefore reads UFC 1-0 instead of debuting. Nothing in
`events` records which promotion a card belongs to.

## Why `is_active = false` is not enough

The owner's concern is borne out by the live schema.

**33 of the 34 views that read `fights` never test `is_active`.** The only one
that does is `v_fight_alert_market`. Among those that don't:
- `v_fighter_consistency` and `v_fighter_cardio_curve`;
- `v_fighter_style`, `v_fighter_finisher` and `v_fighter_results`;
- `fighter_round_stats`;
- every Stat Finder view;
- `v_fight_market_movement`.

A deactivated booking stays in all of them.

**About 40 code paths read `fights` directly:**
- **Pages:** `event.html`, `fighter.html`, `h2h.html`, `index.html` and others.
- **Build scripts:** `build/factor-rates.js`, `build/prerender.js`.
- **Engine and research:** `cfl_engine/features/build_features.py`,
  `cfl_engine/cardio/build_features.py`, the integrity checks and research
  scripts.

Most of them do not filter on `is_active` either.

**`is_active` already means something else.** It means *"this booking was
retired before it happened"* (D-024). DWCS 10.3 *did* happen. Overloading the
flag would make it say two different things.

**Measured reach of the 5 bouts today:**
- **Round-by-round data:** 8 rows in `fight_rounds`.
- **Late-round scores:** 4 rows in `v_fighter_consistency`, for fighters whose
  only recorded fights are on that card.
- **The one-loss fighter:** a UFC record with one extra fight and a last-five
  entry that isn't UFC.

**Every other table holding a `fight_id` has nothing for these 5 ids:** odds,
picks, model locks, snapshots, prop data, user bets, alerts and watchlists. That
covers 22 tables, checked one by one. Only `fight_rounds` references them.

## Options

| | what | reaches every UFC consumer? | changes needed | keeps DWCS history? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | `is_active = false` | **No**: 33 views and most code ignore it | 5 rows | yes |
| B | Add `events.promotion`, then filter `promotion = 'UFC'` in every consumer | Only once every consumer is edited, and every **future** one remembers | ~33 views and ~40 files | yes |
| **C** | **Add `events.promotion`, and move non-UFC bouts into sibling tables `fights_non_ufc` / `fight_rounds_non_ufc` (same columns, same ids)** | **Yes, with no consumer edits**: `fights` once again means UFC fights | 1 migration plus a scraper change | **yes, verbatim, same ids** |

## Recommendation: C

It is the smallest change that is reliable. `fights` already means "UFC
fights" to every view and script in the repo, so restoring that meaning fixes
all of them at once. Every other option leaves a trap for the next person who
writes a query.

1. **Classify cards.** Add
   `events.promotion text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'UFC' CHECK (promotion IN ('UFC','DWCS','RTU','OTHER'))`,
   then backfill `'DWCS'` for DWCS 10.3.
   - It is decided by one shared rule on the event name, the same rule the
     T-083 reconciliation uses:
     - UFC is "UFC …", "Noche UFC …", "The Ultimate Fighter …" or the 2006
       "Ortiz vs Shamrock 3";
     - "DWCS …" or "Dana White's Contender Series …" is DWCS;
     - "Road to UFC …" is RTU.
   - Today the only non-UFC card in `events` is DWCS 10.3.
2. **Preserve, then separate.** In one transaction, with row counts asserted
   before and after:
   - create `fights_non_ufc` as `LIKE fights INCLUDING ALL`, and
     `fight_rounds_non_ufc` the same way from `fight_rounds`;
   - copy in the 5 bouts and their 8 rounds unchanged (same ids);
   - verify the copies match the originals column for column;
   - then delete the originals from `fight_rounds` and `fights`.
   - **The delete is reversible** by copying back: same ids, no foreign key
     points at them.
   - **Grants:** the new tables get the same read grants as `fights`
     (`anon, authenticated`), so DWCS can still be analysed separately.
3. **Prevent recurrence** in `cage-metrics-event-scrapper`:
   - classify each event by name before upserting;
   - write non-UFC events with their `promotion` set, and their bouts and
     rounds to the `_non_ufc` tables;
   - never write them to `fights`.
   - UFCStats now lists DWCS episodes on its "completed events" page, so
     **season-10 episodes will keep arriving** until this lands.
4. **Detect recurrence:**
   - add an integrity check (`cfl_engine/integrity/checks.py`) that fails if any
     `fights` row sits on an event whose `promotion <> 'UFC'`;
   - keep the T-083 reconciliation workflow as a manual audit.

**Effect on the site after C:**
- Galanti shows "No UFC fights yet".
- The 4 DWCS-only late-round rows disappear.
- No UFC fight changes anywhere.
- `v_fighter_ufc_record` (T-084) counts UFC bouts by construction.

## What this plan does not touch

- No UFC result is modified.
- `fighters.ufc_*` is left as it is (T-084 keeps it, unread).
- `is_active` keeps its D-024 meaning.

## Needs a decision

- **Approve C**, or choose B.
- **The delete-after-copy in step 2.** It removes rows from `fights` and
  `fight_rounds`, so it is an L3 data change under `CRITICAL_GATES.md` even
  though it is reversible.
- **Scheduling:**
  - The scraper change (step 3) is in another repository. It should land
    **before** the migration runs. Otherwise a full (non-incremental) `events_scraper.py` run re-imports the card.
  - Until then:
    - `weekly_refresh.py` re-reads completed events from the last 14 days
      (`RECENT_EVENT_DAYS`), and `events_scraper.py`'s `INCREMENTAL` mode
      re-reads the 3 most recent.
    - DWCS 10.3 is outside both windows, so once moved it will not come back
      on its own. A **new** DWCS episode inside either window would still be
      imported into `fights`.
