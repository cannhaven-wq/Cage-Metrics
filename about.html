About

Built for bettors, not touts

The story behind Cannon Fight Lab and why it exists.

Cannon Fight Lab is a UFC analytics platform built for one purpose: giving MMA bettors the hard data to make their own informed picks.

Every other corner of MMA betting is dominated by hot takes, picks pages, and Discord touts charging $50/month to tell you what to bet. The actual data — strike accuracy by division, age advantage, reach gap win rates, post-loss form, finish patterns — is either locked behind premium subscriptions, scattered across a dozen forums, or never surfaced at all.

This site does one thing: it consolidates that data layer into a single, fast, searchable place. Then it adds an analytics layer on top — derived insights you can use to evaluate any matchup, build any parlay, and form your own conviction.

What's in the database

4,506 fighters · the substantially complete UFC historical roster
788 events · every UFC card from UFC 1 through next week
8,853 fights · with results, methods, and round-by-round detail in progress

All data is updated nightly from public UFC sources, so the counts above grow weekly as new cards land. The platform doesn't sell picks. It doesn't run a Discord. It doesn't tell you who to bet on. It gives you the numbers and gets out of your way.

The three products

CFL is three things. The Factor Lab (free) tests which fight stats actually predict winners and which just repeat the betting line — so far, age is the only factor that survives that test. Fight IQ is a model that never sees a betting line: judged only on fights it had never seen, it calls 63.6% of them right from tape stats alone — within five points of just picking the Vegas favorite (68.4%), without ever looking at Vegas. Value is a model built to beat the opening odds; its simulated point-in-time record lives in the Proof Center, and its live record started July 2026 and is 0-1 so far — the first settled bet lost, and it's on the board like everything else.

The core tools

  • Next event card — the upcoming UFC card with every matchup's key stats side-by-side at a glance.
  • Fighter database — search any UFC fighter, see their full UFC record, career averages, and complete fight history.
  • Head-to-head compare — pick any two fighters, see every stat lined up, with the statistical edge highlighted on each row.
  • Stat finder — derived analytics across the full database. Younger fighter win rates, stance matchups, reach advantages, finish patterns by division.
  • Parlay builder — pull fighters from upcoming cards, enter your sportsbook odds, see combined payout and implied probability.

Who built this

Cannon Fight Lab is built and maintained by Reed Cannon. It's an independent project — no investors, no parent company, no editorial agenda beyond "show the data clearly and let bettors decide."

The site is in active development. Some features are still rolling out. Some data points (per-fight striking accuracy, control time, head-strike ratios, round-by-round cardio drop-off) are coming as additional scraping completes. If you find a bug, a fighter that's missing, or a stat that looks wrong, reach out.

What this site is not

This is not a sportsbook. We don't take bets. We don't issue picks. We don't guarantee outcomes. The stats, edges, and projections shown here describe historical patterns — they don't predict any individual fight. A 56% historical edge still means 44% of the time the pattern doesn't hold.
