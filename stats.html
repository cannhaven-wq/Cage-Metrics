Which stats actually predict fights — and which just repeat the betting line. Every number computed live from the full UFC history, with the sample size attached.

Does it survive the market?

Every factor below is scored against — UFC fights. The first column is how often the fighter it favours actually won. The second column is the one that matters: the same rate across only the fights the betting market priced as a coin flip — both men inside ±140 at close. Hold the market flat, and see what the factor still knows.

A factor that looks strong in column one and collapses in column two isn't telling you about the fight. It's telling you who the favourite was, which you could already read off the odds for free. Every stat here is rebuilt from fights that had already happened when the fight being scored took place — a fighter's career record contains the result we're trying to predict, so we never use it. Nothing is shown below 100 fights.
