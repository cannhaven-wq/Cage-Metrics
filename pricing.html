What we are not selling. Sportsbook prices come from The Odds API and fighter statistics come from UFCStats. Those are inputs to CFL's research, not the product — we are not in the business of charging you for access to somebody else's feed. What CFL adds is the capture schedule, the provenance, the vig-free consensus, the movement history, the matchup comparisons and the factor testing. That is what a paid tier would ever be for.
And no picks. CFL used to sell a model forecast and a betting flag with a suggested stake. We tested whether that forecast beat the price the market was offering, could not show that it did, and took it off the product rather than keep charging for it. The engine still runs privately against a locked public record. If it ever earns its way back, the evidence goes up first. See what we have tested →

FAQ

What does an account get me right now?
Bet tracking in My Book, and the Cannon Card Brief. Every research surface on the site is open without one.

When does CFL Pro launch?
No date. The features listed above are not built. When they are, the expected starting price is around $10–15 a month, and the free board does not shrink to make room for them.

Will the free tier ever go away?
No. Card Lab, Market Lab, the Factor Lab and the Proof Center stay free. A record you can check without paying us is the whole point.

Do you sell picks?
No, and we do not publish a model forecast either. CFL shows you what the market is doing and what is measurably true about a matchup. What you do with that is yours.

Are the prices live?
No. They are the last prices we captured, and every one is shown with its book count and its capture time. Check the sportsbook before you act on anything here.
