Just create a free account. Premium features are unlocked for every account holder during beta — no card, no trial timer. When paid pricing launches, we'll give plenty of notice and grandfather early users where we can.

No firm date. We're keeping Premium free during beta to build a track record across multiple events first. Expect 1-3 months minimum.

No. The free tier is permanent. We may add features to it over time but the core data stays free.
