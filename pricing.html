FAQ

When does Premium launch?
Pricing and launch date will be announced to the waitlist first. Estimated 1-3 months out, after we collect a track record across multiple events.

Will the free tier ever go away?
No. The free tier is permanent. We may add features to it over time but the core data stays free.

Why are you charging if there are no closing odds yet?
Premium is what funds adding closing odds, expanded coverage, and the pre-card brief. You're paying for the synthesis layer — calibrated edge factors and detailed analysis — not for predictions of unknowable future events.
