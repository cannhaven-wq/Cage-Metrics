How to use this page

What this is

Each line below is a fight on an upcoming UFC card. For every fight, every model we've trained gives its own predicted win probability for each fighter and compares it to what the betting market is offering. When the model thinks a fighter is meaningfully more likely to win than the market price implies, the row gets flagged ★ — that's a candidate bet.

How to read a row

Model
Which version of our predictor made the pick. v3 is the most advanced; v1 the simplest.
Pick
The fighter the model favors more than the market does.
Opener
The American odds that fighter opened at (BFO consensus across multiple sportsbooks).
Op p
What the opener implies about that fighter's win probability.
Model p
What our model predicts the fighter's win probability actually is.
Edge
(Model p − Opener p). The gap. Bigger gap = better bet, in theory.
Stake
How much we recommend you bet, given your bankroll. See "Bet sizing" below.
★
The model flags this row as a bet (edge ≥ 5%).

3-step quick start

  1. Set your bankroll. Type your total betting capital into the Bankroll box. Stake amounts scale automatically.
  2. Check "Consensus only." A consensus pick is when 2+ different model versions independently flag the same fighter. The page hides everything else — these are the highest-confidence bets of the card.
  3. Bet the recommended Stake on the recommended Pick. We recommend Polymarket for execution (no sportsbook vig, no banning winners).

Bet sizing — the "Kelly" thing

Kelly is a formula that tells you the bet size that mathematically maximizes long-term growth, given your model's win probability and the odds offered. Bigger edges = bigger stakes. We default to Half Kelly because Full Kelly has brutal short-term variance — half-Kelly captures ~75% of the long-run growth with ~25% of the swings.

Max bet cap (default 5%) is a safety net against any single bet wrecking the bankroll if the model is wrong about one fighter. Even at 0% Kelly value, you'll never be exposed beyond this cap on one bet.

Edge thresholds — when to bet

We use edge ≥ 5% as the default "would_bet" threshold. Lower thresholds catch more bets but include more marginal ones; higher thresholds (7–10%) are more selective with better per-bet ROI but fewer opportunities. The model card stats above show how each threshold performed historically.

Honest caveats. This is an experimental research tool, not a tout service. The model's backtest shows ~+5–10% ROI on the 2023+ hold-out, but real-world ROI is typically 30–60% lower due to liquidity friction and book limits. Confidence intervals on the ROI estimate cross zero — meaning a losing month or two is normal, not a sign the strategy is broken. Never bet money you can't afford to see swing 30–40% down before recovering. Always gamble responsibly. 1-800-GAMBLER.
