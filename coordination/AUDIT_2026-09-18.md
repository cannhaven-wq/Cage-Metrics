## The short version

Cannon Fight Lab has a research and integrity apparatus that is genuinely
better than the product around it. The pre-fight record is append-only and
trigger-enforced, the leakage gate publishes that the engine *loses* to the
closing line, the frozen-file register has a tripwire that has been verified to
bite, and `proof.html` is an unusually honest evidence surface.

Three things undercut it:

1. **The public claims contradict the internal measurements.** The homepage
   title, meta description, OG card, hero headline and onboarding steps all
   assert things this repository's own artifacts refute — including a closing-
   price claim that CLV-001 exists specifically to forbid.
2. **The test suite does not run anywhere.** 624 tests, 4,565 subtests and 71
   JS assertions guard the frozen hashes, the CLV gate, the proof-gate
   separation, the DUR-002 conformance guarantee and the coordination
   invariants. All pass. Only one unittest module is wired to CI. The
   tripwires are real and nothing pulls them.
3. **`edges.js` publishes factor strengths with no artifact behind them.** The
   site's own market-controlled measurement points against two of them and
   vindicates the one that was retired — though, as the corrected evidence
   artifact records, neither pointing-against is yet an exact test of the rule
   as shipped.

None of the three is hard to fix. All three are load-bearing for credibility.

### The largest statistical inconsistency is in `edges.js`

`edges.js` assigns hardcoded strengths to two factors. The site's own
market-controlled measurement — `factor-rates.json`, regenerated on the 6-hour
cron — disagrees with both, and vindicates the factor that was retired.

| Factor in `edges.js` | Claimed | `factor-rates.json` market-even | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `recordEdge` | **60 / 65 / 70 / 72 %** | **55.1 %**, n=136, CI **46.8 – 63.3** | `lean` |
| `tdDefEdge` | **52.5 / 54 / 56 %** | **49.3 %**, n=298, CI **43.7 – 55.0** | `proxy` |
| `cardioEdge` | retired Aug 2026 at 50.2 % | — | correctly retired |
| *age* — **retired May 17** | n/a | **56.7 %**, n=868, CI **53.4 – 59.9** | **`real`** — the only one |

Those constants trace to no artifact. `CFL_RESEARCH_STATE.md`'s own rule — *"a
number does not appear on a CFL surface unless it can be traced to an artifact
named here"* — is violated by the file `CLAUDE.md` calls the single source of
truth for verdicts. Cardio was given the market-control treatment and retired;
record and TD-defence never were.

**Corrected 2026-09-18 after review.** The table's comparison is close but not
exact, and the difference is load-bearing. `factor-rates.json` applies **no**
`willHaveWrestling()` gate where `edges.js` fires its takedown-defence factor
only when somebody in the fight wrestles, and it bands the **raw** record gap
where `edges.js` bands a **smoothed** one.

So the figures above are evidence against these factors **in their generic
form**. They are not a measurement of the rules as shipped, and this audit does
not treat them as one. The exact, gated test is a separate piece of work.

**Ownership, as of 2026-09-18:** factor evidence is **not this audit's
workstream**. A dedicated factor-evidence line (FE-001) owns the exact
market-controlled measurement and will publish the authoritative artifact. What
this audit contributes is the finding that prompted it — `edges.js`'s published
ranges trace to no artifact — and nothing here should be read as the verdict on
those factors.

**Mitigating, and it matters:** `computeEdges` has no production consumer.
`index.html` no longer loads `edges.js`; `event.html` loads it but calls only
`cardioFor`. It survives in `research/*.js` and in the `cfl-snapshotter`, whose
cron has been dead since 2026-05-29.

**Not mitigating:** `edges.html` is a live, indexed explainer that publishes
those ranges as **Active**. The claim ships even though the code does not run.

## 3. Launch UX — first-time bettor walkthrough

**Landing.** Strong. Hero, top-edge panel, full card with expandable per-fight
detail, good loading states and a genuinely thoughtful failure state
(*"Card data unavailable — check ad blocker / tracking prevention"* after 15s).

Problems: the hero promise is a market claim the data contradicts; the
"How to use CFL before a card" steps name the drivers as *"age, cardio,
takedown defense"* — **three for three stale**; and the `track-note` paragraph
is ~180 words of dense stats prose against `COPY_STYLE.md`'s 8-second target.

**Card → fight.** The row shows pick, confidence, a Model / Market / Edge
strip, "Why the model likes it" and "Why the model might be wrong". The red
flags are the best-written copy in the product.

But **the "why" is not the model's reasoning.** The pick comes from a
49-feature gradient-boosted model (`p_cal`); the bullets come from
`fight-insights.js`, an independent heuristic over cardio tier, age, reach and
record. Nothing on screen says so, and a reader will reasonably assume the
bullets are what produced the number.

**Fight → detail.** "View matchup" goes to `cfl.eventUrl(eventId)` — the whole
card, no fight anchor. There is no per-fight permalink anywhere in the product,
despite 63 `/preview/<a>-vs-<b>-<id>.html` pages existing that are exactly that.

**→ Track record.** Two competing pages; the better one is unreachable from the
nav.

**→ Email capture.** Present on **3 of 27** pages — `index`, `stats`,
`track-record`. Absent from `event`, `fighter`, `props`, `proof`, and from all
4,620 generated pages, i.e. absent from every page a search visitor lands on.

**Mobile.** `_shared.css` carries 5 media queries. `track-record.html` — the
primary trust page — injects **8 tables and wraps 1** in an `overflow-x`
container, and that one carries `min-width: 520px`. At 390px that is horizontal
page overflow on seven tables. `stats.html` and `predictor.html` have tables
with no wrapper at all.

**Jargon still on screen:** Brier, calibration, vig, walk-forward,
out-of-sample, Kelly, ROI, point-in-time, `p_cal`. `COPY_STYLE.md` has an
explicit swap table for most of these; it is not applied.

**Dead click paths.** `track-record.html`'s primary CTA and `predictor.html`
both point at `card-lab.html`, a redirect stub → `/`. Two hops on the main
conversion path.

### Four structural problems

1. **Orphan pages.** No internal link anywhere points at `/preview/` or
   `/card/`. 71 real content pages are reachable only through the sitemap, so
   they carry no internal link equity.
2. **Canonical conflict.** Event 4433 exists at three URLs — `/card/…-4433`
   (self-canonical), `/e/…-4433` (canonical → `event.html?id=4433`), and
   `event.html?id=4433`. Two assert canonicality for the same content.
3. **The 5,347 stubs may index nothing.** Each carries
   `<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="0; …">` — a zero-second refresh, read
   as a redirect — pointing at a JS-only page with no server-rendered body.
   (`CLAUDE.md` describes a "~120ms JS redirect"; the shipped stub is an
   instant meta-refresh. The doc and the artifact disagree.)
4. **No measurement.** Plausible is on 21 of 27 root pages, on **0** of the
   4,620 generated pages, and **not on `proof.html`**. There is currently no way
   to tell whether any of the SEO work produces a visit.

### Stale and duplicated

Committed batches under `social/` are for events 117 / 118 / 2563 / 2619 /
2620 / 2626 (July 2026) while the live card is 4433; the generator is
`workflow_dispatch`-only so they never refresh. Three overlapping generators —
`social-engine.js`, `social-post.js`, `draft-post.js` — read the same table and
render it three ways. The committed copy leads on cardio as a pick signal and
says "4 of 6 models agree", both retired framings.

**`data/exports/` has never been committed.** `export.yml` runs daily and its
stated purpose is that "a daily snapshot is the only history we get" for
`prop_projections` and `v_fight_odds_consensus`, both of which are destructive
by design. No commit in the repository's history touches `data/`. Either the
workflow has never succeeded or its commit step never fires — worth one look,
because the thing it protects against is unrecoverable.

## 8. Recommended priority order

The order below was proposed on 2026-09-18 and the owner then set the working
order recorded in HANDOFF.md. Both are kept: the reasoning is worth more than
the ranking.

1. **Reconcile public claims with measured evidence.** Every day the meta
   description ships "graded at real closing prices" is a day the SEO work
   amplifies a claim CLV-001 forbids.
2. **Wire the test suite into CI.** One workflow, no product risk, and it
   protects every change after it.

   of work in the audit.
4. **Remeasure `record` and `td_def`; re-examine age.** Closes the largest
   statistical inconsistency. Read-only.
5. **Label the explanation layer.** One sentence removes a real
   misrepresentation.
6. Email capture on the pages search visitors actually land on.
7. Internal-link `/preview/` and `/card/`; resolve the canonical conflict; add
   analytics to generated pages.
8. Mobile table wrappers.
9. Per-fight permalinks.
10. Prop Board graded record.
11. Migrations, once the Railway precondition clears.

## 10. Method, so this can be re-run

Repo state `1e0c3d3`; `origin/main` and the working branch identical. Findings
were produced by reading the repository, the committed build output
(`factor-rates.json`, `sitemap.xml`, `feed.xml`, `/f/`, `/e/`, `/preview/`,
`/card/`), and the frozen artifacts under `cfl_engine/harness/` and

**Corrected 2026-09-18, same day.** The first version of this document reported
168 Python tests. That was `pytest tests/` only, and it undercounted by 456.
The correction makes the finding stronger, not weaker: the untested-in-CI
surface includes the frozen model path, not just the coordination layer.

No live database read was possible. Any finding that depends on current row
