Where these numbers come from

What changed, and why

The research is public, including the part that did not work.
See the full record →

We used to publish a model forecast for every fight. We tested whether that forecast beat the price the market was offering. It did not — our own benchmark has the engine landing inside a few points of the closing line without ever seeing one, which is a respectable model and is not an advantage you can bet. So in September 2026 the forecast came off the public product. Nothing was deleted: the engine still runs, still writes a locked record before every bell, and is still graded in the open on the Proof Center, wins and losses at the same size. If it ever earns its way back onto the card, the evidence will be published before the number is.

What replaced it is what we were confident in all along: the market itself. Sportsbook prices, captured on a schedule, stored with their source and timestamp and never overwritten — so we can show you what a line was, what it is, and what moved in between. Alongside it the Factor Lab, which tests the fight stats everyone cites and reports which ones still hold up once you control for what the betting market already knew. Most of them do not, and it says so.

How to work a card with CFL

1
Read the consensus
The median sportsbook price with the book's cut taken out — what the market actually thinks, in plain probability.
2
Check what moved
How far the line has travelled since CFL first saw the fight priced by three or more sportsbooks, measured only across the books quoting then and now. Movement is information about the market, not a signal to follow.
3
See where the books split
How far apart the sportsbooks are right now, and who is posting the best number on each side.
4
Check the matchup facts
Age, reach, pace, takedown offence and defence, late-round output — with the Factor Lab's verdict on whether each one has ever mattered.
5
Decide for yourself
CFL does not tell you who wins and does not claim an advantage over the price. Research is the product; the bet is yours.
