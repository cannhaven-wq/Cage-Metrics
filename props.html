How to read it. The SIG number is the model's central projection for significant strikes landed assuming the fight reaches the scorecards — the cleanest number for an over/under, because it depends only on each fighter's own pace and the opponent's output-allowed, not on how long the fight lasts. The bar shows the middle-60% range (20th–80th percentile). Takedown P(1+)/P(2+) already fold in the chance of an early finish.
Duration is a v1 baseline. The finish / distance split is the league-average 3-round curve, the same for every fight for now — not yet fighter-adjusted. Strike and takedown projections are fully fighter-specific.
Model output for research, not betting advice. Projections are estimates with real uncertainty — thin-tape fighters especially. No posted prop lines were used; these are the model's own numbers.
