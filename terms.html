3. What CFL is, and what it is not

Nature of the Service

We show the market and the matchup. We don't tell you who to bet on.

CFL is a UFC research and market-intelligence publication. It shows sportsbook prices with their source and the time we captured them, a consensus price with the bookmaker's margin removed, how a market has moved, comparisons between two fighters, and tests of which fight statistics have historically mattered.

  • No picks and no betting advice. CFL does not publish predicted winners, picks, betting recommendations or suggested stakes. Nothing on the Service is advice to place, or not to place, any bet.
  • No promise of profit. We make no claim that you will profit from anything on the Service, or that anything on it beats the betting market.
  • Prices are not live. Every price is the last one we captured. It is shown with its age and how many sportsbooks it comes from, and it may have changed since. Always check the sportsbook before you act.
  • "Best price" is mechanical. It is the best number in the data we hold, ordered by the American price and by nothing else. No sportsbook can pay to change that order. CFL carries no affiliate links today; if it ever does, that ordering will not change and the links will be labelled.
  • Archive pages are history. Some pages document a forecast CFL used to publish and has since taken off the product. They are kept as a public record of what was tested, including what failed. They are not recommendations.
  • We are not a sportsbook. CFL does not accept wagers, hold funds or process bets.

4. Where the data comes from

Third-party sources

We build on public fight data and sportsbook prices. They can be wrong or late, and so can we.

CFL's research uses third-party data, including fight statistics from UFCStats, sportsbook prices from The Odds API, and fighter details from public sources such as UFC.com and Wikidata. That data is an input to CFL's work; what CFL adds is how and when prices are captured, where each one came from, the consensus, the movement history, the matchup comparisons and the factor testing.

We work to keep the data accurate, but it can contain errors, gaps or delays, at the source or introduced by us. We do not guarantee that any figure is complete, correct or current. If you find an error, please tell us.

CFL is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Ultimate Fighting Championship, Zuffa LLC, TKO Group Holdings, UFCStats, The Odds API, or any sportsbook. UFC and fighter names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the events and people described.

9. Emails and alerts

What we send, and how to stop it

We only email you about things you asked for, and every email lets you stop.

  • The Cannon Card Brief is an optional email before each card. We will ask you to confirm your address before sending it, and we will not send the Brief to an address that has not confirmed. Every Brief has an unsubscribe link.
  • Alerts are emails you set up yourself on fights you follow — for example, when a price reaches a number you chose, or a market moves. Alerts report what the market did; they are not recommendations. By the time you read one the price may have changed. CFL will not send an alert built on a market comparison it would refuse to show on the site, and alerts can be late or not sent at all — do not rely on them for anything time-critical.
