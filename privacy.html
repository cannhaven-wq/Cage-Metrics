Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and what you can do about it.

In plain English

We collect the minimum amount of data we need to run the site. We do not sell your data. We use cookies for analytics and to make affiliate links work. If you sign up for our email list, you can unsubscribe at any time. For questions, use the contact link below.

1. Who we are

The party responsible for your data

This site is operated by Cannon Fight Lab LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company ("Cannon Fight Lab," "CFL," "we," "us," or "our"). Contact information is listed in Section 12.

2. What we collect

Categories of data

Automatically collected. When you visit cannonfightlab.com, our analytics provider records limited technical information: your IP address (truncated where possible), browser type and version, operating system, the pages you view, how you arrived at the site (referrer URL), and approximate location at the city level. This information is used to understand how the site is used and to detect abuse.

Voluntarily provided. If you create an account, subscribe to our email list, or contact us, we collect the information you give us — typically email address, and any message you send.

Cookies and tracking. We use cookies and similar technologies for site functionality, analytics, and affiliate link tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may not work correctly without them.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, payment card information, or any other financial account data. We do not collect data from anyone we know to be under 13 years old.

3. How we use your data

Purposes
  • To operate and improve the site
  • To send you email content you have signed up for, including pre-card briefings and product updates
  • To measure which content and features are useful
  • To track affiliate referrals to licensed sportsbook partners
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, or technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not sell your data to third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

What we set and why

The site uses three categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary. Required for the site to function (e.g., remembering whether you have accepted the cookie banner). Cannot be disabled
  • Analytics. Help us understand how visitors use the site. We use a privacy-respecting analytics provider that does not build cross-site profiles
  • Affiliate tracking. When you click a sportsbook affiliate link, a cookie may be set by the sportsbook so they can credit your eventual signup to us. We do not control these third-party cookies

You can opt out of non-essential cookies through the cookie banner on first visit or by clearing cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third parties

Who else sees your data

We share data only with service providers necessary to run the site, and only the data they need:

  • Hosting and infrastructure. The web host that serves the site and the database that stores it
  • Analytics provider. Aggregated traffic data only
  • Email service provider. If you subscribe, your email address and engagement signals are stored with our email platform
  • Sportsbook affiliate partners. When you click an affiliate link and create an account with a sportsbook, that sportsbook may share with us limited information about the referral (typically: a clicked-through-yes-or-no signal and account creation status) for the purpose of paying our commission

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, by valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CFL, our users, or others.

6. Your rights

What you can ask us to do

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a portable copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent for processing that depends on consent
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share for cross-context behavioral advertising, but you can confirm this)

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact information in Section 12. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

California residents: You have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what categories of personal information have been collected about you and the right to delete that information. EU/UK residents: You have rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR including the rights listed above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Data retention

How long we keep your data

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form for up to 24 months. Email subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe or until your account is inactive for an extended period. Contact-form messages are retained for up to 12 months unless they relate to an ongoing matter.

8. Data security

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

9. International transfers

Cross-border data flow

Cannon Fight Lab is based in the United States. If you access the site from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. By using the site, you consent to this transfer.

10. Children

Under 18

This site is intended for adults aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

Updates over time

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The "Last updated" date at the bottom of the page indicates the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced on the site for a reasonable period.
