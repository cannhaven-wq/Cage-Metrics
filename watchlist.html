How often we email you

We send one email per round of checks, however many of your alerts fired in it — never one per alert.

Quiet hours are not set up yet, deliberately. We do not know what time zone you are in, and a guessed quiet window silences the alerts you asked for at exactly the hours you most wanted them. Until we ask you for a time zone, the cooldown and the daily cap above are what keep this quiet.

What an alert will never do. It will not tell you who to back, it will not suggest a stake, and it will not claim CFL knows something the market does not. It reports what happened to a price. Every number we email you carries how many sportsbooks were behind it and when we captured it, and the prices are the last ones we captured — never live. Check the sportsbook before you act on anything.

And it will stay quiet rather than guess. A movement alert needs at least three sportsbooks quoting at both ends of the comparison. Below that we have no honest figure, so you hear nothing — that is the alert working, not failing.
